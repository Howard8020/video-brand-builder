# Video Brand Builder — Intro Video Script

*Built using the Video Brand Builder pipeline to demonstrate the platform itself.*

## Brief
- **Client:** Video Brand Builder
- **Service Line:** SaaS Platform
- **Goal:** Brand awareness / product demo
- **Audience:** Small business owners who need video ads but have no production team
- **Tone:** Confident, warm, direct — speaks to the pain point, shows the solution
- **Platform:** Instagram / TikTok (9:16)
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds (4 segments × 6s)
- **Visual Mode:** Mixed (on-camera spokesperson + screen capture)
- **CTA:** "Start your first script at videobrandbuilder.ai" (on-screen + spoken)

## Gate 1 — Script

### Segment 1 — Hook (6s)
**Purpose:** hook
**Spoken:** "Your video ads shouldn't sound like everyone else's."
**On-screen text:** [Your ads, your voice]

### Segment 2 — Problem (6s)
**Purpose:** benefit
**Spoken:** "Most AI tools give you 10 clips that don't match — different voices, different looks, different vibes."
**On-screen text:** [Inconsistent AI output — faded, disjointed clips on screen]

### Segment 3 — Solution (6s)
**Purpose:** example
**Spoken:** "Video Brand Builder locks your script first, stamps your brand into every scene, and gives you prompts that actually go together."
**On-screen text:** [Split screen: chaotic clips on left → cohesive branded clips on right]

### Segment 4 — CTA (6s)
**Purpose:** cta
**Spoken:** "One ad, done right. Start your first script free at videobrandbuilder.ai."
**On-screen text:** [videobrandbuilder.ai — Start free]

## Gate 2 — Scene Treatments

### Scene 1 — Hook
**Action:** A confident founder/spokesperson stands in a bright, modern studio (clean white background, teal accent wall). They look directly at camera, warm smile. As they say the line, a subtle lower-third appears with "Video Brand Builder" logo.
**Camera:** Medium close-up, slightly low angle (empowering). Slow push-in over the 6 seconds.
**On-screen text:** Lower third: "[Your ads, your voice]" in navy bold type — fades in at second 2.

### Scene 2 — Problem
**Action:** The spokesperson steps to the side. The screen behind them shows a split comparison: 3 disjointed AI-generated clips playing simultaneously (differing lighting, different "actors," mismatched backgrounds). Visual is chaotic, unpolished.
**Camera:** Widen to reveal the screen. Dolly right as spokesperson gestures toward it.
**On-screen text:** Overlay on comparison grid: "Clip 1 ≠ Clip 2 ≠ Clip 3" in red, fades out.

### Scene 3 — Solution
**Action:** Screen transitions to a clean view of the Video Brand Builder dashboard — the review screen showing "Gate 1 — Script" with continuity fields. A cursor clicks through the pipeline: Generate → Lock Script → Approve Scenes → Export. Each step highlights briefly.
**Camera:** Static screen capture, slight zoom on the continuity fields to emphasize they're filled in.
**On-screen text:** "Lock. Approve. Export." — fades in sequentially with each click.

### Scene 4 — CTA
**Action:** Back to spokesperson in the studio, now holding a smartphone showing the exported prompts screen. They gesture toward the phone. Logo appears in bottom-right corner.
**Camera:** Medium shot, rack focus from face to phone screen, then back.
**On-screen text:** [videobrandbuilder.ai — Start free] in brand teal, persists to end.

## Continuity Profile
- **Spokesperson:** Friendly founder type, early 40s, smart casual (navy blazer or crisp white shirt)
- **Delivery:** Warm, confident, unhurried — conversational, not scripted
- **Setting:** Bright studio with white and teal (#2DD4BF) accent wall, clean lines
- **Lighting:** Soft key light + fill, warm temperature — professional but not corporate
- **Brand:** Clean whitespace, navy/teal palette, lower-third logo on first and last segments
